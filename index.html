AFINORA

A practice room for string players. It starts as a real-time tuner and carries on into everything you do once the strings are in tune.

Works with a USB audio interface

What it does

Technique training

Play a scale shape against the metronome and every note is graded as it arrives: clean, late or missed. Pitch and timing are measured separately, so you know which one failed.

Studio

Import a backing track, mark a section, loop it and slow it down. Record yourself over it as audio or video, then save the take or share it.

Tuner

Real-time detection on the device, ten instruments, over 30 standard and alternate tunings plus your own, and six A4 references from 432 to 442 Hz.

Chords and scales

20 scales and 20 chords drawn on the neck of your instrument in your tuning, with a fullscreen view and the chords that belong to the key.

Metronome

An audio engine that does not drift. 30-300 BPM, tap tempo, signatures from 2/4 to 12/8, and any beat of the bar accented or muted.

Ear training

Notes and intervals, answered on your real instrument rather than by tapping a button.

Why a wired signal matters

Grading a note requires hearing it exactly. An unamplified electric guitar is not a signal a detector can read reliably, and a room microphone drops notes as soon as there is noise. Plug into a USB audio interface and Afinora detects it, compensates its latency and tells you it is connected.

Tested with the Fender Mustang Micro Plus; other compatible USB audio interfaces should work the same way. Acoustic instruments can train through the phone microphone instead.

Instruments

Guitar, 7-string guitar, bass, ukulele, Venezuelan cuatro, violin, viola, cello, mandolin and banjo.

Free and Pro

Afinora opens with 5 days of full access. After that the free version keeps one instrument of your choice, the major and minor scales, and 5 Studio recordings. Afinora Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and unlocks everything permanently.

Private by default

No account, no ads, no tracking. Audio used for tuning and training is analysed on the device as it arrives and is never recorded or uploaded. Studio recordings stay on your phone unless you share them yourself. See the Privacy Policy.
