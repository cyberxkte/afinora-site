Afinora

Real-time tuner for guitar and string instruments.

Afinora helps musicians tune quickly and accurately with real-time pitch detection, a clean cents indicator, standard and alternate tunings, reference tones, and ear training.

Features

  • Real-time pitch detection using the microphone
  • Clear cents indicator for accurate tuning
  • Support for guitar, bass, violin, ukulele, mandolin, banjo, cello and more
  • Standard and alternate tunings
  • Custom tunings
  • Adjustable A4 reference pitch
  • Reference tones and ear training
  • Dark and light themes
  • No external ads

Privacy-friendly

Afinora processes microphone audio on your device to detect pitch. The app does not record, store, upload, sell or share microphone audio.
