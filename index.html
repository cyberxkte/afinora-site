Technique training

It listens as you play and grades every note for pitch and timing

You pick a scale, set a tempo and play it against the metronome. The pitch detector listens and grades every note separately the moment it arrives: in tune and on time, late, or not detected. Pitch and timing are measured apart, so when you finish you know which specific notes to work on instead of guessing. The notes travel along the neck with one colour per finger and one lane per string, and you play them on the instrument.

01 · Tuner

Reads every note exactly

Real-time pitch detection, processed on the device itself. Over thirty standard and alternate tunings, plus the ones you build yourself. A reference tone on any string and six A4 references, from 432 to 442 Hz. Reads an unplugged electric guitar.

02 · Chords and scales

The whole neck, in your tuning

Twenty scales and twenty chords, drawn where you actually play them: up to 24 frets, with a fullscreen view. Notes or degrees, chord voicings including barre shapes, and the chords that belong to the key you are looking at. From there, a scale goes straight into training.

03 · Metronome

Set the tempo, pick the bar, play

Don't know the BPM? Tap it in and Afinora works it out. 30 to 300 BPM, bars from 2/4 to 12/8, and subdivisions down to sixteenths — the ones that help at slow tempos, where the space between beats is what throws you off. Tap any beat of the bar to accent it or mute it. It is the same click technique training measures your timing against, and it is complete in the free version.

04 · Studio

Record yourself over the backing track

Import a backing track, mark the section that gives you trouble, loop it and slow it down. Record yourself over it as audio or video, save it to your gallery or share it. Recordings are files on your phone: the app never uploads them.

05 · Ear training

You hear the note and play it on your instrument

Afinora plays a note or an interval, you find it on the neck and play it, and the detector checks whether you got it right. You answer on the instrument, not by picking an option on screen.

How it listens

How Afinora listens to your instrument

Grading note by note only works if the signal coming in is clean. These are the three cases, and which one applies to you.

Acoustic: through the microphone

Acoustic guitar, violin, cello, ukulele, mandolin, cuatro. The phone's microphone is enough to tune and to train.

Electric: through a USB interface

An unamplified electric is not a signal a detector can read reliably, and a room microphone drops notes as soon as there is noise. With an interface, Afinora detects it, compensates its latency and tells you it is connected.

It is all processed here

Audio used for tuning and training is analysed on the device as it arrives. It is never recorded or uploaded.

Tested with the Fender Mustang Micro Plus, and works with other compatible USB audio interfaces. Fender and Mustang Micro Plus are trademarks of Fender Musical Instruments Corporation; Afinora is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Fender.

Private from the start

No account, no ads, no tracking

There is no sign-up and no login: you open the app and play. There are no third-party ads and no analytics of our own. Audio used for tuning and training is analysed on the device as it arrives and is never recorded or uploaded. Studio recordings stay on your phone unless you share them yourself.
