Afinora Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Afinora respects your privacy. This app is designed to work entirely on-device and does not collect, store, or share personal information.

Microphone Access

Afinora uses microphone access exclusively for real-time pitch detection and tuning functionality.

Audio is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded, transmitted, stored, or shared with external servers.

Data Collection

Afinora does not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Email addresses
  • User accounts
  • Location data
  • Audio recordings
  • Usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Tracking information

Third-Party Services

Afinora currently does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Afinora does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated in future versions of the app.
