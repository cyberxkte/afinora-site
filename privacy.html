Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Afinora is designed to be privacy-friendly. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Microphone access

Afinora uses microphone access only to detect the pitch of your instrument in real time. Microphone audio is processed locally on your device.

Afinora does not record, store, upload, sell, or share microphone audio.

Personal data

Afinora does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information directly from you.

Purchases

Afinora may offer an optional in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Afinora does not receive or store your payment card details.

Analytics and advertising

Afinora does not show external ads. The app does not use microphone audio for advertising or tracking.

Children’s privacy

Afinora does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: cyberxkte@gmail.com
